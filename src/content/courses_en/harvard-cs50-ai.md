## 📌 What is this course?
Part of Harvard University's celebrated **CS50** computer science series, **CS50 AI** explores the core foundational concepts and algorithms at the foundation of modern artificial intelligence.

Taught by David J. Malan and Brian Yu, this course dives beyond superficial library calls. Students build AI engines from scratch in Python—including **adversarial game trees (Minimax/Alpha-Beta pruning) for Tic-Tac-Toe, pathfinding with A* search, Bayesian probabilistic inference, constraint satisfaction, and convolutional neural networks with TensorFlow**.

All video lectures, slides, comprehensive text notes, and autograded projects are **100% free to audit** for anyone worldwide.

## 📚 Core Syllabus
1. **Search**: DFS, BFS, Greedy Best-First, A* Search, Minimax & Alpha-Beta Pruning
2. **Knowledge**: Propositional Logic, Model Checking, Inference Engines, Resolution
3. **Uncertainty**: Probability, Conditional Independence, Bayes' Rule, Markov Models
4. **Optimization**: Local Search, Hill Climbing, Simulated Annealing, Linear Programming
5. **Learning**: Supervised Learning, Nearest-Neighbor, Perceptron, SVM, Overfitting Prevention
6. **Neural Networks**: Artificial Neural Networks, Backpropagation, Computer Vision with TensorFlow
7. **Language**: Syntax, Semantics, Word Embeddings, Transformers, Natural Language Processing
