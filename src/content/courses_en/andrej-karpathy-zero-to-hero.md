## 📌 What is this course?
Created by **Andrej Karpathy** (former Director of AI at Tesla and OpenAI founding researcher), **Neural Networks: Zero to Hero** is arguably the most respected hands-on deep learning curriculum on the internet.

Rather than relying on high-level abstractions in PyTorch or Keras, Karpathy starts with a tiny scalar autograd engine called **micrograd**, demonstrating exactly how gradients flow through computational graphs. The series progresses through character-level language modeling, multi-layer perceptrons (MLP), manual backpropagation math, batch normalization debugging, and culminates in **building a generative transformer (nanoGPT, following the GPT-2 architecture) from scratch** and pretraining it on Shakespeare text.

---

## 🎯 Who is this for?
- Developers who use PyTorch or Hugging Face but feel frustrated not understanding what happens inside the tensor graph.
- Engineers eager to master the exact mathematical and algorithmic implementation of Self-Attention and Transformers.
- Anyone who wants to observe a world-class AI researcher write, test, and debug neural network code line by line.

## 📚 Core Syllabus
1. **The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation**: Building micrograd from scratch
2. **The spelled-out intro to language modeling**: Building makemore part 1 (Bigram model)
3. **Building makemore Part 2 (MLP)**: Following the Bengio et al. 2003 paper
4. **Building makemore Part 3 (Activations & Gradients)**: BatchNorm and Kaiming initialization
5. **Building makemore Part 4 (Manual Backpropagation)**: Deriving tensor calculus by hand
6. **Building makemore Part 5 (WaveNet)**: Hierarchical convolutions for longer context
7. **Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out**: Implementing Multi-Head Attention and Transformers
8. **Let's build the GPT Tokenizer**: Understanding Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) from scratch
