## 📌 What is this learning path?
Google Cloud's official curriculum introducing foundational concepts of **Generative AI, Large Language Models, Attention mechanisms, and Transformer architectures**.

Upon completing the core modules and passing the assessment quizzes, learners earn an **official Google Cloud Skill Badge** that can be added to LinkedIn and resumes.

---

## 🎯 Who is this for?
- Non-engineers, product managers, and developers seeking a crystal-clear understanding of generative AI.
- Professionals looking for credible, verifiable certifications from Google.
- Engineers planning to build on Google Cloud's Vertex AI ecosystem.

---

## 📚 Pathway Curriculum
1. **Introduction to Generative AI**: Defining GenAI and how it differs from traditional ML.
2. **Introduction to Large Language Models (LLMs)**: How foundation models are trained and fine-tuned.
3. **Introduction to Responsible AI**: Ethical frameworks, bias mitigation, and Google's 7 AI Principles.
4. **Generative AI Fundamentals Assessment**: Cumulative quiz awarding the official digital skill badge.
5. **Introduction to Image Generation**: Exploring Diffusion models and generative image synthesis.
6. **Encoder-Decoder Architecture & Attention**: Demystifying how transformers process sequential information.

---

## 💡 Learning Tips
- **Unlimited Retakes**: Quizzes require a 75% score to earn the badge, but multiple retakes are allowed if you miss a question.
