## 📌 What is this course?
Taught by **Professor Andrew Ng** (founder of Google Brain, Coursera, and DeepLearning.AI), **Stanford CS229: Machine Learning** is the most celebrated collegiate machine learning curriculum in the world.

This course goes far beyond surface-level tooling to teach the **rigorous mathematical foundations, statistical learning theory, and algorithmic proofs** of machine learning. You will master linear regression, logistic regression, Generalized Linear Models (GLMs), Support Vector Machines, Kernel methods, the EM algorithm, Principal Component Analysis (PCA), and Reinforcement Learning (MDPs and Q-Learning).

Stanford provides all official lecture slides, in-depth mathematical PDF notes, and high-definition YouTube recordings completely free to the public.

---

## 🎯 Who is this for?
- Students and engineers seeking a deep theoretical and mathematical mastery of machine learning algorithms.
- Researchers and candidates preparing for rigorous machine learning engineering and research scientist technical interviews.
- Anyone who wants to learn directly from Andrew Ng's iconic blackboard derivations.

## 📚 Core Syllabus
1. **Supervised Learning**: LMS algorithm, Normal equations, Logistic regression, Newton's method
2. **Generative Learning Algorithms**: Gaussian Discriminant Analysis (GDA), Naive Bayes classifier
3. **Support Vector Machines**: Functional and geometric margins, Lagrange duality, KKT conditions, Kernel trick
4. **Learning Theory**: Bias/variance tradeoff, Union bounds, VC dimension, Regularization
5. **Unsupervised Learning & Clustering**: k-Means, Mixture of Gaussians, Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm
6. **Dimensionality Reduction**: Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Singular Value Decomposition (SVD), ICA
7. **Reinforcement Learning**: Markov Decision Processes (MDP), Bellman equations, Value iteration, Q-Learning
