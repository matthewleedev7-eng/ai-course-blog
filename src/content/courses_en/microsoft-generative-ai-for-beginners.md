## 📌 What is this course?
A 21-lesson open-source curriculum created by **Microsoft's Cloud Advocates team**, designed to take developers from absolute beginners to capable builders of generative AI applications.

Each lesson includes **visual sketch notes, video overviews, Jupyter notebooks, sample code, and knowledge-check assignments**.

---

## 🎯 Who is this for?
- Developers who want a comprehensive, university-grade curriculum created by Microsoft.
- Engineers looking to master **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**, Vector Databases, and Agent architectures.
- Teams designing enterprise-ready AI applications with security and compliance considerations.

---

## 📚 Key Curriculum Modules
- **Lessons 01~03**: GenAI & LLM Architecture Overview.
- **Lessons 04~06**: Advanced Prompt Engineering Patterns.
- **Lessons 07~08**: Building Text Generation and Chat Applications.
- **Lessons 09~10**: Search Applications with Vector Databases.
- **Lessons 11~12**: **Deep Dive into RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**.
- **Lessons 13~15**: Multimodal Applications (Vision & Audio).
- **Lessons 16~18**: Fine-Tuning Open Source Models & Autonomous Agents.
- **Lessons 19~21**: Enterprise AI Safety, Compliance, and DevOps.
